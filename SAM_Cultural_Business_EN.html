01 · The cultural & heritage sector

Culture is becoming
an economy.

Saudi Arabia is investing in culture as part of its national transformation. Heritage is no longer only something to preserve — it is something people build careers and businesses around.

01 · HERITAGE

A deep cultural base.

Crafts, dress, architecture, food, music and stories carry knowledge passed down through generations — and real value for today's audiences.

02 · CHANGE

A sector opening up.

New cultural programmes, events and audiences are creating more room for cultural products, services and experiences.

03 · ENTERPRISE

Small businesses carry it.

Much of this activity comes from individual makers, studios and small enterprises. Their growth is how cultural value reaches the market.

04 · How it works

Nine steps from
“where am I?” to “what's next.”

Each step builds on the one before it, so every recommendation can be traced back to something the business told SAM.

01 · PROFILE

Business profile

Category, stage, product or service, audience, current challenges and capabilities.

02 · ASSESS

Readiness assessment

Six dimensions, from value proposition to IP and rights awareness.

03 · IDENTIFY

Development gaps

SAM highlights the areas where the business most needs support.

04 · RECOMMEND

Personalised recommendations

Prioritised, practical advice based on the profile and the gaps.

05 · MATCH

Experts & resources

Relevant types of experts, resources and support pathways.

06 · PLAN

Development roadmap

Actionable next steps in a clear order: now, next, later.

07 · ASK

08 · LEARN

Business insights

Aggregated, privacy-protected view of sector development needs.

09 · TRACK

Progress & reports

Readiness results and a development report the owner can keep.

09 · Responsible AI

Useful guidance.
Honest limits.

WE DO

Explain and guide

Use transparent scoring for the assessment, and plain-language explanations that trace back to what the user entered.

WE DO NOT

Replace experts or decide for you

SAM does not give legal, financial or IP rulings, and does not guarantee commercial results. The owner makes every decision.

DATA

Privacy first

Business information stays with the user. Any sector insight would be aggregated and anonymised. This prototype stores nothing.

Transparency note: in this prototype the score is a simple average, the recommendations are rule-based, Ask SAM replies are scripted, and all experts and insights are sample content. No live AI, real expert matching or external integration is running.
